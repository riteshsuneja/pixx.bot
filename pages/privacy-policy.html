1.0 DATA COLLECTION

PIXX.BOT collects information necessary to provide our capture services. This includes:
• Identity Data: Name and contact details provided via booking forms.
• Event Data: Images and videos captured via our System Modules.
• Technical Data: IP addresses and browser behavior via Microsoft Clarity and Google Analytics.

2.0 PURPOSE OF PROCESSING

We process data to:
• Deliver high-fidelity visual assets to event guests.
• Provide lead-generation analytics to our brand partners.
• Optimize website performance and system stability.

3.0 THIRD-PARTY ANALYTICS

We utilize Microsoft Clarity and Google Analytics to capture how you interact with our website through behavioral metrics and heatmaps. This data is used for site optimization and security. For more information on how Microsoft collects and uses your data, visit the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

4.0 DATA RETENTION

Visual assets captured during events are stored for a default period of 30 days unless otherwise agreed upon with the brand partner. Analytical data is retained in accordance with third-party provider policies.
